# FoodRescue — User Requirements

## 2.4 User Requirements

### 2.4.1 Donor Requirements

UR-DONOR-01: The donor shall be able to create and manage an account.

UR-DONOR-02: The donor shall be able to log in and log out of the system.

UR-DONOR-03: The donor shall be able to manage their profile information.

UR-DONOR-04: The donor shall be able to create surplus-food listings.

UR-DONOR-05: The donor shall be able to provide relevant food details including quantity, preparation time, expiry or use-by time, and pickup location.

UR-DONOR-06: The donor shall be able to view their active food listings.

UR-DONOR-07: The donor shall be able to view requests submitted for their listings.

UR-DONOR-08: The donor shall be able to approve or reject eligible requests.

UR-DONOR-09: The donor shall be able to cancel an available listing where permitted.

UR-DONOR-10: The donor shall be able to confirm food handover.

UR-DONOR-11: The donor shall be able to view donation history.

UR-DONOR-12: The donor shall be able to view relevant impact statistics.

### 2.4.2 Recipient Requirements

UR-RECIP-01: The recipient shall be able to register and maintain their profile information.

UR-RECIP-02: The recipient shall be able to log in and log out of the system.

UR-RECIP-03: The recipient shall be able to view available surplus-food listings after satisfying applicable eligibility requirements.

UR-RECIP-04: The recipient shall be able to search and filter available food listings.

UR-RECIP-05: The recipient shall be able to view detailed information about a food listing.

UR-RECIP-06: The recipient shall be able to submit a request for available food.

UR-RECIP-07: The recipient shall be able to specify the quantity required when submitting a request.

UR-RECIP-08: The recipient shall be able to track the status of submitted requests.

UR-RECIP-09: The recipient shall be able to view approved allocations.

UR-RECIP-10: The recipient shall be able to confirm receipt of delivered food.

UR-RECIP-11: The recipient shall be able to view request and receipt history.

### 2.4.3 Volunteer Requirements

UR-VOL-01: The volunteer shall be able to register and maintain relevant availability information.

UR-VOL-02: The volunteer shall be able to log in and log out of the system.

UR-VOL-03: The volunteer shall be able to view assigned pickup and delivery tasks.

UR-VOL-04: The volunteer shall be able to accept or reject assignments where permitted.

UR-VOL-05: The volunteer shall be able to view pickup and delivery details.

UR-VOL-06: The volunteer shall be able to update pickup status.

UR-VOL-07: The volunteer shall be able to confirm collection of food.

UR-VOL-08: The volunteer shall be able to update delivery status.

UR-VOL-09: The volunteer shall be able to confirm delivery.

UR-VOL-10: The volunteer shall be able to view completed assignments and task history.

### 2.4.4 Administrator Requirements

UR-ADMIN-01: The administrator shall be able to log in securely.

UR-ADMIN-02: The administrator shall be able to view and manage registered users.

UR-ADMIN-03: The administrator shall be able to approve or reject recipient registrations requiring verification.

UR-ADMIN-04: The administrator shall be able to manage donor accounts.

UR-ADMIN-05: The administrator shall be able to manage volunteer accounts.

UR-ADMIN-06: The administrator shall be able to monitor food listings.

UR-ADMIN-07: The administrator shall be able to remove invalid food listings.

UR-ADMIN-08: The administrator shall be able to monitor food requests and allocations.

UR-ADMIN-09: The administrator shall be able to monitor pickup and delivery activities.

UR-ADMIN-10: The administrator shall be able to review and resolve reported issues or disputes.

UR-ADMIN-11: The administrator shall be able to manage food categories and relevant system data.

UR-ADMIN-12: The administrator shall be able to view system statistics and reports.

UR-ADMIN-13: The administrator shall be able to monitor successful and failed redistribution activities.

UR-ADMIN-14: The administrator shall be able to manage relevant notifications and system settings.
